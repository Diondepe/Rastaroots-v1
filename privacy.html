The Details

Who is responsible for your data.

Rasta Roots Limited (trading as RastaRoots) is the controller of your personal information. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 17278421. Registered office: 651A Mauldeth Road, Chorlton, Manchester, M21 7SA. Contact: hello@rastarootslife.com.

Why we use your information (our lawful basis)

  • To take, deliver and support your order: because we need it to fulfil our contract with you.
  • Emails such as The Rootz Letter, offers and library access: only with your consent. You can unsubscribe from any email at any time.
  • Analytics, keeping the site secure and preventing fraud: our legitimate interests in running and improving the business.
  • Keeping tax and accounting records: because the law requires it.

Who we share it with

We only share what each service needs to do its job: Shopify (our shop and checkout) and its payment partners; Mailchimp, run by Intuit (our email service); Google Analytics (website statistics); and our courier, to deliver your order. Some of these providers store data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law. We never sell your personal information.

Cookies and your browser

We only set Google Analytics cookies if you choose Accept analytics in Cookie settings; they help us understand how people use the site. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer. We do not use advertising cookies. When you join the free Rootz Library, we save a small note in your browser so the guides stay unlocked when you come back. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for six years for tax and accounting reasons. Email subscriber details are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive a copy to take elsewhere. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email us and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
