Cinnamon

Cinnamomum verum & C. cassia

Sweet • Woody • Warming

The sweet bark of Caribbean Christmas, Grenada’s spice gardens and every winter kitchen. How to make cinnamon tea, Ceylon vs cassia, what research really shows and why the type matters.

Quick answer

Is cinnamon tea good for you?

Cinnamon tea is made by simmering the dried inner bark of cinnamon trees in water. It is naturally sweet, warming and caffeine-free. Trials of cinnamon for blood sugar have given mixed results, so it is not a diabetes treatment. The type matters: Ceylon cinnamon contains very little coumarin, while cassia cinnamon, the most common type in UK shops, contains much more, which can affect the liver in large amounts.

Side effects & who should take care

  • Normal cooking and tea amounts of cinnamon are fine for most people.
  • If you use cinnamon a lot, every day, choose Ceylon cinnamon.
  • About 2 g of cassia cinnamon a day reaches the safe coumarin limit for a 60 kg adult.
  • Ask a pharmacist before using cinnamon supplements, especially with diabetes or liver problems.

Cinnamon At A Glance

What is cinnamon?

Cinnamon is the dried inner bark of several trees in the laurel family, rolled into quills or ground to powder.

Botanical Names Cinnamomum verum (Ceylon), C. cassia (cassia)
Family Lauraceae (laurel family)
Part Used Inner bark
Character Sweet, woody, warm
Native Range Sri Lanka, India, southern China

Cinnamon, Caribbean spice and the evidence

Cinnamon is one of the world’s oldest traded spices, and it is part of Caribbean food from sorrel and Christmas cake to porridge and cocoa tea.

Grenada, known as the Spice Isle, grows cinnamon alongside nutmeg and cloves.

As a tea it is sweet and warming. Health claims, especially for blood sugar, are not proven.

01 • Know The Bark

Two kinds of bark, one familiar spice.

Cinnamon is made by peeling the inner bark from young cinnamon branches and drying it until it curls into quills, or sticks.

Ceylon cinnamon (Cinnamomum verum) has thin, papery layers rolled together, a light colour and a delicate, sweet flavour.

Cassia cinnamon (Cinnamomum cassia and related species) has one thick, hard layer, a darker colour and a stronger, spicier taste. Most cinnamon sold in UK supermarkets is cassia.

Cassia naturally contains much more coumarin than Ceylon cinnamon.

Same name. Different bark. Know which one you are using.

02 • Cinnamon Benefits

What is cinnamon tea good for?

Cinnamon is a beloved warming spice. Its health reputation, especially for blood sugar, runs ahead of the evidence.

The honest picture: mixed results, not a diabetes treatment.

Some trials of cinnamon supplements in people with type 2 diabetes have reported small improvements in blood sugar, while others found no clear effect. Doses, types and study quality vary widely. Cinnamon tea is not a substitute for diabetes care, and high doses of cassia carry a coumarin risk.

Clinical Research

Blood sugar

Cinnamon supplements have been tested in type 2 diabetes with mixed results.

Traditionally Associated

Warmth and digestion

Across the Caribbean and beyond, cinnamon is used in warming drinks for cold weather and after meals.

Evidence Context

Type matters

Studies use different cinnamon species and doses, making results hard to compare.

04 • Caribbean Heritage

An ancient spice that found a home in the islands.

Cinnamon is native to Sri Lanka and southern India (Ceylon cinnamon) and southern China and Southeast Asia (cassia). It was traded across the ancient world for thousands of years.

European colonial powers fought over the cinnamon trade, and spice trees were later planted in the Caribbean.

Today cinnamon grows in Grenada, the Spice Isle, alongside nutmeg and cloves, and it flavours Caribbean sorrel, Christmas cake, cocoa tea and porridge.

07 • What Modern Research Says

Popular claims. Mixed evidence.

Cinnamon is widely promoted for blood sugar, but the research does not give a clear answer.

Clinical Trials

Inconsistent results

Trials in type 2 diabetes vary in dose, type of cinnamon and quality, with mixed findings.

Key Limitation

Supplements, not tea

Most studies use capsules of powder or extract, not cinnamon tea.

Safety Benchmark

Coumarin limit

EFSA and Germany’s BfR set a tolerable daily intake of 0.1 mg of coumarin per kg of body weight.

10 • How To Make Cinnamon Tea

How to make cinnamon tea.

Sticks give the clearest, sweetest tea. Ceylon sticks are best if you drink it often.

Cinnamon stick tea

Break 1 cinnamon stick (about 5 cm) into a small pan. Add a few slices of fresh ginger or a strip of orange peel if you like.

Add 300 ml of water, bring to the boil, then simmer gently for 10 to 15 minutes.

Strain into a mug. The tea is naturally sweet, so taste before adding honey.

In a hurry? Stir ¼ teaspoon of ground cinnamon into hot water, leave for 5 minutes and pour carefully, leaving the sediment behind.

11 • Cinnamon Side Effects & Safety

A warming spice, with one thing to know.

Coumarin in cassia.

Cinnamon used in food and the occasional cup of tea is fine for most people.

Cassia cinnamon contains coumarin, which can harm the liver in sensitive people at high intakes. Germany’s BfR says an adult weighing 60 kg reaches the tolerable daily intake with about 2 g of cassia cinnamon a day, roughly a teaspoon.

If you use cinnamon every day in large amounts, choose Ceylon cinnamon, which contains very little coumarin.

Cinnamon supplements are often cassia and can be much stronger than food. Ask a pharmacist first, especially if you take diabetes or blood-thinning medicines, have liver problems, are pregnant or breastfeeding.

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication, preparing for surgery or managing a medical condition, speak to a pharmacist or doctor before using cinnamon supplements or large daily amounts, and never use cinnamon instead of prescribed diabetes treatment.

12 • Questions People Ask About Cinnamon

Cinnamon tea questions, answered clearly.

Straightforward answers to the questions people search for most.

Is cinnamon tea good for you?
Cinnamon tea is a warming, naturally sweet, caffeine-free drink. Research on blood sugar is mixed, so enjoy it as a drink rather than a treatment.
How do you make cinnamon tea?
Simmer one cinnamon stick in 300 ml of water for 10 to 15 minutes, then strain. Add ginger or orange peel for extra warmth.
How do you make cinnamon tea with powder?
Stir ¼ teaspoon of ground cinnamon into a mug of hot water, leave for 5 minutes, then pour off the tea and leave the sediment.
What is the difference between Ceylon and cassia cinnamon?
Ceylon (“true”) cinnamon has thin, papery layers and very little coumarin. Cassia has one thick layer, a stronger taste and much more coumarin. Most UK supermarket cinnamon is cassia.
Does cinnamon tea lower blood sugar?
Some trials of cinnamon supplements found small improvements in type 2 diabetes and others found none. It is not a diabetes treatment.
How much cinnamon is safe a day?
For cassia cinnamon, Germany’s BfR says about 2 g a day reaches the safe coumarin limit for a 60 kg adult. Ceylon cinnamon contains very little coumarin.
Can I drink cinnamon tea every day?
Yes, in normal amounts. If you drink it daily, Ceylon cinnamon is the safer choice.
Is cinnamon safe in pregnancy?
Cinnamon in normal food amounts is fine. Avoid cinnamon supplements during pregnancy unless your midwife or doctor advises them.
Which Caribbean island grows cinnamon?
Grenada, the Spice Isle, grows cinnamon alongside nutmeg, cloves and other spices.
Does cinnamon tea contain caffeine?
No. Cinnamon tea is naturally caffeine-free.
