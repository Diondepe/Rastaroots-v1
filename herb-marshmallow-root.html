Marshmallow Root

Althaea officinalis

Mild • Sweet • Silky

The original marshmallow: a soothing, silky root used for sore throats and dry coughs. How to make it properly with cold water, what is recognised and who should take care.

Quick answer

What is marshmallow root good for?

Marshmallow root (Althaea officinalis) is rich in mucilage, a slippery gel that coats the mouth and throat. European regulators recognise its traditional use for mouth or throat irritation with a dry cough and for mild stomach discomfort, based on long use. It is best made by soaking in cold water. It is not the same as marshmallow sweets.

Side effects & who should take care

  • It may slow how your body absorbs other medicines: take it at a different time.
  • See a doctor if a throat or cough problem lasts more than a week.
  • Not enough is known about safety in pregnancy and breastfeeding.
  • It may lower blood sugar slightly; take care with diabetes medicines.

Marshmallow Root At A Glance

What is marshmallow root?

Marshmallow is a tall, soft-leaved plant in the mallow family that grows in salt marshes and damp ground. Its roots are full of soothing gel.

Marshmallow, Egypt and the evidence

Marsh mallow grows wild in salt marshes, including in Britain, and across Europe, North Africa and western Asia.

The first marshmallow sweets were made from its root sap. Today’s sweets contain none.

European regulators recognise its traditional use for sore throats with dry coughs and mild stomach discomfort.

01 • Know The Root

The original marshmallow.

Marsh mallow grows up to 1.5 metres tall, with soft, velvety grey-green leaves and pale pink flowers.

Its thick, pale roots are full of mucilage, a gel that swells in water and coats the throat and stomach lining.

Cold water draws out the mucilage best. Boiling extracts more starch and less gel.

It is in the same family as hibiscus and okra, which are also slippery.

A root made of soothing silk.

02 • Marshmallow Root Benefits

What is marshmallow root good for?

Marshmallow is one of the few herbs with recognised traditional uses in Europe.

The honest picture: recognised traditional comfort, limited trials.

The European Medicines Agency’s herbal committee concluded that marshmallow root preparations can be used for mouth or throat irritation with a dry cough, and for mild stomach discomfort, based on long-standing use. A few small studies of syrups and drops suggest it may ease dry cough, but strong trials are limited.

04 • African & World Heritage

From Egyptian sweets to British salt marshes.

Marsh mallow grows wild across Europe, North Africa and western Asia, and in salt marshes around the British coast.

Ancient Egyptians are said to have made a honey sweet with mallow sap.

In 19th-century France, confectioners whipped the root sap with sugar and egg white into the first marshmallows.

10 • How To Make Marshmallow Root Tea

How to make marshmallow root tea.

Marshmallow root works best as a cold infusion.

Cold-infused marshmallow root

Put 1 tablespoon of dried, chopped marshmallow root in a jar.

Add 250 ml of cold water, cover and leave for 4 hours or overnight.

Strain. The liquid will be slightly thick and silky.

Drink it cool, or warm it gently without boiling. Add honey if you like.

11 • Marshmallow Root Side Effects & Safety

Gentle, with a medicine timing rule.

Medicines, blood sugar, pregnancy and when to see a doctor.

Marshmallow root is generally well tolerated.

Medicines: its mucilage may slow how your body absorbs other medicines. Take marshmallow root at a different time, ideally at least an hour apart.

Blood sugar and pregnancy: it may lower blood sugar slightly, so take care with diabetes medicines. There is not enough information on safety in pregnancy or breastfeeding.

When to see a doctor: if throat irritation or a dry cough lasts more than a week, or stomach discomfort lasts more than two weeks.

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication, preparing for surgery or managing a medical condition, speak to a pharmacist about timing marshmallow root with your regular medicines.

The Rootz Library is educational and does not diagnose, treat or replace medical advice.

12 • Questions People Ask About Marshmallow Root

Marshmallow root questions, answered clearly.

Straightforward answers to the questions people search for most.

What is marshmallow root good for?
It has recognised traditional use for throat irritation with a dry cough and mild stomach discomfort.
How do you make marshmallow root tea?
Soak 1 tablespoon of dried root in 250 ml of cold water for 4 hours or overnight, then strain.
Why use cold water for marshmallow root?
Cold water draws out the soothing mucilage best.
Is marshmallow root the same as marshmallow sweets?
No. The first marshmallows were made from the root, but modern sweets contain none.
Can marshmallow root affect medicines?
It may slow their absorption. Take it at a different time, ideally an hour apart.
Is marshmallow root safe in pregnancy?
There is not enough information. Ask your midwife or GP.
Is marshmallow root good for a sore throat?
It is traditionally used to soothe throat irritation. See a GP if it lasts more than a week.
Does marshmallow root contain caffeine?
No. It is caffeine-free.
Can children have marshmallow root?
Licensed products have age limits. Check the label or ask a pharmacist.
Where does marshmallow grow?
In salt marshes and damp ground across Europe, North Africa and western Asia, including Britain.
