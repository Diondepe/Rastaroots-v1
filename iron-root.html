Meet The Guardian

Strength is what remains after the struggle.

The Iron Root Guardian represents endurance developed over a lifetime. His strength is not presented as youth, appearance or instant power. It is the visible result of discipline, hard work, recovery and the refusal to abandon his roots when the journey became difficult.

“Built on ancient roots. Tested by time. Made for the journey.”

The Story Behind The Blend

Endurance is built before it is ever required.

Iron Root was created for the people who continue when motivation disappears. Its identity comes from earthy sarsaparilla, warming ginger and the softer leafy foundation of moringa.

The name is not a promise of physical performance or a medical claim. It represents rooted strength: the ability to remain disciplined, recover, adapt and continue moving forward.

The Iron Root Ritual

Brew. Recover. Continue.

Iron Root is a ritual for people committed to the long journey. It creates a deliberate pause between effort and what comes next: space to recover, reflect and prepare to continue.

STEP 01

Prepare

Place one Iron Root sachet into your cup and prepare it according to the brewing instructions supplied with the collection.

STEP 02

Recover

Step away from noise and allow the blend to develop. Treat rest as part of the work rather than evidence that the work has stopped.

STEP 03

Listen

Play the RastaRoots Tea Ritual playlist and create space for a slower rhythm after effort, training or a demanding day.

STEP 04

Continue

Identify the next disciplined action. Not everything needs to be completed today, but the journey should continue with intention.
