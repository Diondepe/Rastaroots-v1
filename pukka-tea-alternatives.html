Your guide

What to know before you choose

A different approach to herbal tea

RastaRoots is a Manchester-built British-Caribbean brand with five blends inspired by Caribbean herbal traditions. Ingredients are sourced from around the world. This is a discovery guide, not a claim that one brand is healthier or better than another.

If you enjoy ginger blends

Roots Fire combines ginger, lemongrass and mint. Expect warming spice with fresh herbal notes. For another ginger-based direction, Iron Root adds sarsaparilla and moringa for a deeper earthy profile.

If you enjoy floral or mint infusions

Empress Bloom combines hibiscus, mint and moringa for a floral-tart cup. Cool Mind uses mint, lemongrass and hibiscus for a fresher direction. Ingredients and flavour are more useful comparison points than broad wellness claims.

If you want a more adventurous bitter tea

Bush Doctor uses cerasee, bitter melon and moringa. It draws inspiration from bitter bush-tea traditions while remaining a modern RastaRoots blend, not a medicinal remedy.

How to compare products fairly

Check the complete ingredients, sachet count, price, allergens, preparation directions and current delivery terms for any tea you are considering. Recipes and prices change; compare current labels rather than relying on generalisations about competitors.
