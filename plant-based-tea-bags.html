Your guide

What to know before you choose

What are botanical herbal infusions?

Herbal infusions use plant materials such as ginger root, mint leaves, hibiscus and lemongrass. They are different from conventional black or green tea made with Camellia sinensis. RastaRoots builds five blends around botanical flavours and Caribbean-inspired heritage.

Are plant-based tea bags plastic-free?

Not necessarily. A tea bag can contain plant-derived fibres yet use other materials in its seal, mesh or coating. “Biodegradable”, “compostable” and “plastic-free” are different claims that need evidence about the finished packaging.

The five RastaRoots blends are botanical herbal infusions, and the Founder Discovery Collection contains 25 sachets. This page does not claim the sachet material is plastic-free, home-compostable, biodegradable or certified plant-based; packaging specifications must be confirmed separately.

How to shop more carefully

Check the product packaging and ask the seller about the filter paper or mesh, sealants, outer wrappers and any independent certification. If compostability matters, check whether it requires industrial facilities or is suitable for home composting.
