Rose

Rosa damascena

Floral • Fragrant • Softly Sweet

The fragrant petals of the damask rose, steeped into a delicate pink tea. Rose petal tea vs rose hip tea, how to make it, what research really shows, and the rose valleys of Morocco.

Quick answer

Is rose tea good for you?

Rose tea is made from the dried petals or buds of fragrant roses, usually Rosa damascena (damask rose). It is delicate, floral and caffeine-free. Rose petal tea is different from rose hip tea, made from the fruit. Rose petals have traditional use for mild mouth and throat irritation, but there is little good research on rose tea benefits. Use petals that have not been sprayed.

Side effects & who should take care

  • Rose tea is gentle and likely safe for most people.
  • Only use roses grown for food: florist and garden roses are often sprayed.
  • Not enough is known about medicinal amounts in pregnancy and breastfeeding.
  • Rose hip is a different plant part with different research.

01 • Know The Petal

Petals, buds or hips?

Rose petal tea uses dried petals of scented roses. Rose bud tea uses whole dried buds, which give a slightly stronger flavour.

The damask rose (Rosa damascena) is the classic tea and perfume rose.

Rose hip tea is made from the red fruit left after the flower fades. It is tangy rather than floral, and it is a different product.

Rose water is the scented water left after distilling petals for rose oil.

Petal, bud, hip or water. Same plant, very different cups.

02 • Rose Benefits

What is rose tea good for?

Rose tea is loved for its scent. Its health reputation is mostly traditional.

The honest picture: a fragrant, gentle cup with traditional uses.

European regulators recognise rose petals for traditional use as a mouth rinse for mild mouth and throat irritation and on the skin, based on long use rather than strong trials. Rose petals contain antioxidant compounds studied in the lab. There is little good research on drinking rose tea for mood, skin or periods.

04 • African & World Heritage

From Morocco’s Valley of Roses to your cup.

The damask rose has been grown for perfume and rose water for centuries in Persia, Turkey and across North Africa.

In Morocco, the Dadès Valley around Kelaat M’Gouna is known as the Valley of Roses. Families pick the petals at dawn each spring and hold a rose festival to celebrate the harvest.

In Britain, roses fill gardens, and rose hip syrup was made from wild hips during the Second World War as a source of vitamin C.

10 • How To Make Rose Tea

How to make rose tea.

Rose is delicate. Use water just off the boil and do not over-steep.

Rose bud tea

Put 5 to 6 dried food-grade rose buds, or 1 tablespoon of dried petals, in a cup.

Pour over 250 ml of water that has cooled for a minute after boiling.

Cover and steep for 5 minutes, then strain.

Add a little honey, or steep with a few mint leaves for a Moroccan-style cup.

Step 01 • Measure 5–6 buds or 1 tbsp petals.
Step 02 • Pour 250 ml water, just off the boil.
Step 03 • Steep 5 minutes, covered.
Step 04 • Finish Honey or mint to taste.

11 • Rose Side Effects & Safety

Gentle, as long as it is food-grade.

Sprays, pregnancy and rose oil.

Rose petal tea is generally well tolerated.

Use food-grade roses: florist and garden roses are often treated with pesticides. Buy roses sold for tea or cooking.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding: rose in food amounts is fine, but there is not enough evidence on medicinal amounts, so stick to the occasional cup.

Rose oil: rose essential oil is a concentrate for the skin and air, not a drink.

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication, preparing for surgery or managing a medical condition, speak to a pharmacist before using rose supplements or extracts, and ask your midwife about herbal teas in pregnancy.

The Rootz Library is educational and does not diagnose, treat or replace medical advice.

12 • Questions People Ask About Rose

Rose tea questions, answered clearly.

Straightforward answers to the questions people search for most.

Is rose tea good for you?
Rose tea is a gentle, caffeine-free floral drink. Its benefits are mostly traditional, with little human research.
What is the difference between rose tea and rose hip tea?
Rose tea is made from petals or buds and tastes floral. Rose hip tea is made from the fruit and tastes tangy.
How do you make rose tea?
Steep 5 to 6 dried food-grade rose buds, or 1 tablespoon of petals, in 250 ml of just-boiled water for 5 minutes.
Can I use roses from my garden?
Only if they have never been sprayed and you know the variety. Scented, food-grade roses are safest.
Does rose tea contain caffeine?
No. Pure rose tea is naturally caffeine-free.
What is the Valley of Roses?
The Dadès Valley around Kelaat M’Gouna in Morocco, famous for damask roses and its spring rose festival.
Is rose tea safe in pregnancy?
An occasional cup is generally fine, but avoid medicinal amounts and ask your midwife.
What are the benefits of rose water?
Rose water is used in cooking and skin care. Claims for skin benefits are mostly traditional.
Which rose is used for tea?
Usually the damask rose (Rosa damascena), also Rosa gallica and Rosa centifolia.
Does rose tea help period pain?
Rose tea is traditionally used for comfort, but there is not enough good evidence. See a GP about painful periods.
