Founder Pre-Order FAQ

Before you order.

Clear answers for the first RastaRoots Life drop.

01

What is the RastaRoots Discovery Collection?

The Discovery Collection is the first RastaRoots Life founder box. It includes 40 premium herbal tea sachets across 5 signature ritual blends, with 8 sachets of each blend.

02

What is included in the box?

Each Discovery Collection includes 40 sachets, 5 signature blends and access to the official RastaRoots Tea Ritual playlist.

03

When will Founder Pre-Orders ship?

Founder Pre-Orders are expected to ship in September 2026. Founder customers will receive production and dispatch updates by email.

04

How much is the Founder Pre-Order?

The Founder Pre-Order price is £19.99. The expected public launch price is £24.99.

05

Where does checkout happen?

Checkout is completed securely through Shopify. The RastaRoots Life website is used for the brand experience, product information and community story.

06

Can I pay by card or PayPal?

Available payment methods are shown at Shopify checkout. Card and PayPal options may be available depending on the checkout settings at the time of order.

07

Can I cancel my pre-order?

You may request cancellation before dispatch. Please read the Refund Policy or contact us with your order number if you need support.

08

Are these medical products?

No. RastaRoots herbal teas are created for daily ritual, lifestyle and general wellbeing. They are not medicines and are not intended to diagnose, treat or cure any condition.

09

What is the Tea Ritual playlist?

The Tea Ritual playlist is the official RastaRoots music experience. It is designed to pair your brew with rooted rhythms for calm, reflection and connection.

10

How do Founder customers help the Community Fund?

Founder customers help build the foundation of RastaRoots Life. As the brand grows, our goal is to develop the Rootz Community Fund to support youth empowerment, mental wellbeing, heritage and grassroots projects.
