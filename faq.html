What is the RastaRoots Discovery Collection?

The RastaRoots Discovery Collection is a Caribbean-inspired herbal tea gift set featuring five signature herbal infusions. The box contains 25 individually wrapped sachets: five sachets of each blend. Explore the Discovery Collection.

02

What teas and ingredients are included?

The five blends are Roots Fire (ginger, lemongrass and mint), Bush Doctor (cerasee, bitter melon and moringa), Empress Bloom (hibiscus, mint and moringa), Iron Root (sarsaparilla, ginger and moringa) and Cool Mind (mint, lemongrass and hibiscus). Always check the current product packaging for complete ingredient and allergen information.

Are RastaRoots herbal teas caffeine-free?

The five Discovery Collection herbal infusions are described as naturally caffeine-free. Check the individual product labels for the most current ingredients and information.

Where are RastaRoots teas made and is the brand Caribbean-owned?

RastaRoots is a Manchester-founded, Black-owned brand inspired by Caribbean herbal traditions and heritage. Its botanical ingredients are sourced from different parts of the world; Caribbean-inspired does not mean every ingredient is grown in the Caribbean. Read the founder story.

Are RastaRoots herbal teas medicines?

No. RastaRoots herbal teas are food and lifestyle products, not medicines. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, prevent or cure a medical condition. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or managing a medical condition, seek qualified advice about the ingredients before use.
