Refund Policy

Refunds, Cancellations & Returns

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations work for RastaRoots Founder Pre-Orders and future product purchases.

Founder Pre-Order Cancellations

The RastaRoots Discovery Collection is currently available as a Founder Pre-Order.

You may request to cancel your pre-order before your order has been dispatched.

To request a cancellation, contact:

hello@rastarootslife.com

Please include your order number, full name and the email address used to place the order.

01

Before Dispatch

Pre-orders may be cancelled before dispatch, subject to the status of your order.

02

After Dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched, we may not be able to cancel it.

03

Refund Timing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing times depend on your bank or payment provider.

Returns

Returns After Delivery

Due to the nature of tea and consumable products, we can only accept returns where items are unopened, unused and in their original packaging, unless the item is faulty or damaged.

If you wish to request a return, please contact us within 14 days of receiving your order.

Damaged Orders

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, faulty or incorrect, please contact us as soon as possible with your order number and clear photos of the issue.

We will review the issue and offer a suitable resolution where appropriate.

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, opened tea products cannot usually be returned unless they are faulty, damaged or incorrect.

Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout. We cannot issue refunds to a different card, bank account or PayPal account.
