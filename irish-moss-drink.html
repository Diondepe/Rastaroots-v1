Rootz Kitchen • Caribbean Punch

Jamaican Irish Moss

Thick • Creamy • Nutmeg-Spiced

Thick, creamy and spiced: Irish moss is a Caribbean punch made from a seaweed that sets into a gel. A Saturday favourite from Jamaican shops and kitchens.

Get The Recipe ↓ More Drinks

Quick answer

How do you make Irish moss drink?

Rinse 30 g dried Irish moss well, soak it overnight, then simmer in fresh water until soft. Blend it with a little water into a smooth gel, then blend 4 to 5 tablespoons of the gel with 500 ml milk, condensed milk to taste, vanilla and nutmeg. Seaweed can be very high in iodine, so enjoy it as an occasional treat.

The recipe in five steps

  1. Rinse 30 g dried Irish moss and soak overnight.
  2. Rinse again, then simmer in fresh water for 15 to 20 minutes.
  3. Blend with a little water into a smooth gel.
  4. Blend some gel with milk, condensed milk, vanilla and nutmeg.
  5. Chill and serve.

01 • Ingredients

What you need.

01 • Seaweed

30 g dried Irish moss

Sold in Caribbean shops. In Jamaica, "Irish moss" is usually a Gracilaria seaweed rather than the Irish Chondrus crispus.

02 • Milk

500 ml milk

Whole milk, or oat or soya milk for dairy-free.

03 • Sweet

4–6 tbsp condensed milk

Or honey or sugar to taste.

04 • Spice

Vanilla, nutmeg, cinnamon

1 tsp vanilla, ¼ tsp grated nutmeg and a pinch of cinnamon. Some add a spoon of linseed or peanut butter.

02 • Method

Step by step.

Classic Jamaican Irish moss

Rinse the dried moss several times to remove sand and salt, then soak it overnight in plenty of water.

Drain and rinse again. Simmer in about 1 litre of fresh water for 15 to 20 minutes until soft and slippery.

Blend the moss with a little of the cooking water until completely smooth. Cool: it sets into a thick gel. Keep it in a jar in the fridge.

For the drink, blend 4 to 5 tablespoons of gel with the milk, condensed milk, vanilla, nutmeg and cinnamon until smooth and thick.

Taste, adjust the sweetness, chill and serve. Drink it the same day.

03 • Variations

Make it your own.

Dairy-Free

Plant Irish moss

Use oat milk and sweeten with maple syrup or a condensed oat milk.

Peanut

Peanut Irish moss

Blend in a spoonful of peanut butter. Avoid if anyone has a peanut allergy.

Linseed

Linseed and moss

Add a spoonful of linseed (flaxseed) gel, a common Jamaican addition.

Adults

Stout Irish moss

Some add a splash of stout. Contains alcohol.

Pudding

Irish moss pudding

Use more gel and less milk, then chill until set like a pudding.

05 • Heritage

From Irish shores to Caribbean kitchens.

The name comes from Irish seaweed, but the Caribbean drink is its own tradition.

Jamaica

Ireland

Carrageen

In Ireland, carrageen moss has long been used to set milk puddings, and the name travelled with it.

Dried Irish moss and bottled moss drinks are sold in Caribbean shops across Britain.

06 • Make It Safely

Safety, allergens and storage.

Good to know before you make it.

Iodine: Seaweed can be very high in iodine, and too much iodine can affect the thyroid. Enjoy Irish moss as an occasional drink, not daily. If you have a thyroid condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, check with your GP or pharmacist first.

Health claims: You will see big health claims for sea moss online. They are not backed by good evidence, and RastaRoots makes none. Enjoy it as a traditional drink.

Allergens: Contains milk unless made dairy-free. Peanut and stout versions contain peanuts and gluten.

Storage: Keep the gel in a sealed jar in the fridge for up to a week. Drink the blended drink the same day.

07 • Questions People Ask

Your questions, answered.

What is Irish moss?
A red seaweed that sets into a gel when cooked and blended. In Jamaica the dried "Irish moss" sold in shops is usually a Gracilaria species; in Ireland the name refers to Chondrus crispus, or carrageen.
Is Irish moss the same as sea moss?
The names are often used for the same group of red seaweeds. "Sea moss" is the name used in most online products.
What does Irish moss taste like?
On its own it is mild and slightly sea-salty. Blended with milk, nutmeg and vanilla it tastes creamy and sweet, like a thick milkshake.
Can I drink Irish moss every day?
It is best as an occasional drink, because seaweed can contain a lot of iodine. Check with your GP if you have a thyroid condition or are pregnant.
Where can I buy Irish moss in the UK?
Caribbean and African grocers sell dried Irish moss, and some sell ready-made bottled moss drinks.
