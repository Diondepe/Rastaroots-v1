Your guide

What to know before you choose

What makes an infusion caffeine-free?

Black, green, white and oolong teas come from Camellia sinensis and naturally contain caffeine. Herbal infusions use other plants: leaves, flowers, roots and sometimes fruits. A blend made exclusively from caffeine-free botanicals is generally expected to contain no naturally occurring caffeine, but this is not a laboratory test of a finished product.

How to check before you buy

Read the complete ingredient list rather than relying on the words “herbal” or “natural”. Watch for tea leaves, yerba mate, guayusa, kola nut and added caffeine. If you need strict avoidance for medical reasons, seek product-specific verification from the seller.

Choose flavour, not a health promise

For a fresh cup, start with mint and lemongrass. For tart, fruit-like brightness, explore hibiscus. For a warming drink, ginger provides a spicy note. Bitter botanicals such as cerasee suit a more adventurous palate.

A tea ritual for any time

A botanical infusion can be a simple part of a morning or evening routine. Enjoy the aroma, follow preparation directions on the packet and take a moment to slow down. The ritual is about preference and experience, not a promise of better sleep or medical outcomes.

Frequently asked questions

Are all herbal teas caffeine-free?

No. Some products marketed as herbal blends contain caffeinated ingredients. Always read the full ingredient list.

Does RastaRoots use black or green tea leaves?

The five current RastaRoots herbal blends are described using roots, flowers and leaves other than Camellia sinensis. Refer to the current product packaging for the definitive ingredient declaration.

Can I drink herbal tea while pregnant?

Not every herb is suitable during pregnancy. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about the specific ingredients and your circumstances.

Does caffeine-free mean a tea helps with sleep?

No. Caffeine content and effects on sleep are separate questions. RastaRoots does not claim its blends treat sleep problems.
