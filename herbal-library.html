Why The Library Exists

Herbal knowledge deserves more than a product label.

RastaRoots is building beyond tea. The Herbal Library will help people understand the plants behind the blends — what they taste like, where they grow, how communities have traditionally used them and what modern evidence does or does not currently support. Cultural tradition and scientific evidence will be treated as related, but not identical.

Our Approach

Respect the tradition. Respect the evidence.

The Herbal Library will be built around four principles.

01 • Origins

Know the plant.

Botanical identity, where the plant grows and the parts typically used in food or herbal preparations.

02 • Tradition

Know the history.

Explore traditional and cultural use while being clear that historical use is not automatically proof of a modern medical effect.

03 • Evidence

Know what we know.

Modern research will be explained carefully, including uncertainty, evidence quality and areas where more research is still needed.

04 • Ritual

Know your cup.

Understand flavour, aroma, preparation and which RastaRoots rituals feature each ingredient.

The Guardian Of Knowledge

Knowing a plant means knowing more than its benefits.

The Guardian exists to protect context. Plants have histories, flavours, communities, preparation traditions and limitations.

RastaRoots will not reduce generations of plant knowledge to exaggerated wellness headlines. The aim is to make herbal education accessible while respecting both tradition and evidence.

“Before you ask what a plant can do for you, learn where it came from.”

Inside Every Library Entry

More context. Less hype.

Every completed herb entry will follow the same framework.

01

Plant Profile

Botanical name, common names, plant family and the parts commonly used.

02

Origins

Where the plant originates, where it grows today and how it spread between regions.

03

Caribbean Context

Traditional preparations, family practices and cultural references associated with the plant.

04

Flavour & Aroma

How the ingredient actually tastes and what it contributes to a finished infusion.

05

Modern Evidence

A careful overview of relevant contemporary research without turning early findings into unsupported claims.

06

RastaRoots Rituals

Which blends contain the ingredient and how it contributes to their flavour profiles.
