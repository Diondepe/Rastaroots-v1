Know Your Rootz

What is the RastaRoots Herbal Library?

The RastaRoots Herbal Library is an educational collection built around the plants used throughout RastaRoots and the wider herbal traditions that surround them.

Each botanical entry is designed to help separate plant identity, flavour, preparation, heritage, traditional context and modern research rather than reducing an herb to a list of internet “benefits”.

Caribbean and African plant knowledge matters here. Traditional knowledge is respected as traditional knowledge, while modern research is presented as modern research. They are connected without pretending they are the same thing.

The Library is educational and does not diagnose, treat or replace professional medical advice.

The Rootz Standard

Herbal knowledge deserves
more than a product label.

The Library is part of a wider RastaRoots idea: the plants should not disappear behind the product. The culture, history and knowledge systems surrounding them deserve space too.

Plant First

Identity before claims.

A common name is not always enough. Botanical identity, plant part and preparation can change what is actually being discussed.

Heritage Matters

Culture is part of the story.

Caribbean and African plant traditions are not decorative branding. They are part of the history that makes these botanicals meaningful.

Evidence Matters

Research needs context.

Traditional use and modern clinical evidence are not interchangeable. The Library keeps those distinctions visible.

Learn The Whole Plant

More than “benefits”.

Search engines are full of pages promising what an herb can do. The RastaRoots Library is designed to answer a bigger set of questions.

Identity

What is it?

Botanical name, common names, plant part and the basic identity of the herb.

Flavour

What does it taste like?

Understand the aromatic, bitter, tart, earthy, cooling or warming character of the plant.

Heritage

Explore the traditions, places and communities connected to the plant.

Preparation

How has it been used?

Explore tea, food and other preparation contexts relevant to each botanical.

Research

What has been studied?

Look at modern research while keeping study type, preparation and evidence limitations visible.

Herbal Library FAQ

Start here.

The essentials about the RastaRoots Herbal Library, membership and how the botanical guides should be used.

What is the RastaRoots Herbal Library?

The RastaRoots Herbal Library is a botanical education resource exploring herbal tea, plant identity, flavour, preparation, Caribbean and African heritage, traditional knowledge and modern research.

Which herbs are currently in the Herbal Library?

The Founding Eight are ginger, lemongrass, mint, cerasee, bitter melon, moringa, hibiscus or sorrel, and sarsaparilla.

Is the Rootz Library free?

Founding Rootz Library membership is free. Members can access Field Guide 001, botanical knowledge checks, Rootz Points and future Library releases.

What does each botanical guide include?

Botanical entries can include plant identity, flavour, heritage, traditional context, preparation, modern research, evidence limitations, safety information, frequently asked questions and links to relevant RastaRoots blends.

Is traditional use the same as scientific evidence?

No. The Library treats traditional knowledge as an important part of plant history and culture while keeping it distinct from modern scientific and clinical evidence.

Does the Herbal Library provide medical advice?

No. The Herbal Library is educational. It does not diagnose, treat or replace professional medical advice.

How does the Herbal Library connect to RastaRoots tea?

The Library helps you explore botanicals that appear throughout the RastaRoots world and understand more about the plants behind the five tea rituals.

What is Field Guide 001?

Field Guide 001 is a companion resource for the Founding Eight, available to Rootz Library members.
