Meet The Guardian

Stillness is not weakness. It is control.

The Cool Mind Guardian represents the discipline required to step back before reacting. He understands that clarity is rarely found inside constant noise. It appears when the mind has enough space to observe, breathe and choose its next response deliberately.

“You cannot hear your roots when every moment is filled with noise.”

The Story Behind The Blend

The mind needs room to return to itself.

Cool Mind was created as the lightest and most refreshing ritual in the RastaRoots Discovery Collection. Mint provides its cooling character, lemongrass adds bright citrus aroma and hibiscus introduces a gentle floral tartness.

Its name is not a promise to treat stress, anxiety or any medical condition. It represents the value of pausing, reducing unnecessary noise and creating a more intentional end to the day.

The Cool Mind Ritual

Brew. Breathe. Release.

Cool Mind is a ritual for creating separation between the demands of the day and the time that belongs to you. It invites a slower pace, fewer distractions and a deliberate transition into rest.

STEP 01

Prepare

Place one Cool Mind sachet into your cup and prepare it according to the brewing instructions supplied with the collection.

STEP 02

Breathe

Notice the mint and citrus aroma as the infusion develops. Take a few slow breaths and allow the pace of the moment to change.

STEP 03

Listen

Play the RastaRoots Tea Ritual playlist and replace unnecessary noise with music selected for reflection and connection.

STEP 04

Release

Identify what does not need to follow you into the next part of the day. Put it down, even if only for the duration of the cup.
