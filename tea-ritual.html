What Is A Tea Ritual?

Tea already asks you to slow down. We made that moment the point.

A tea ritual is a deliberate way of preparing and drinking tea that gives attention to the process as well as the cup. RastaRoots Tea Ritual combines herbal tea preparation, music and intentional time through five signature botanical blends.

It is not presented as a traditional Japanese, Chinese or other formal tea ceremony. It is a modern RastaRoots framework for helping everyday herbal infusions become more meaningful moments in your routine.

The RastaRoots Ritual Method

Brew. Play. Pause. Root.

Tea Ritual does not require complicated equipment or strict rules. It is a simple framework for giving the preparation and the moment more attention.

Step 01

Brew

Start with the cup. Prepare your chosen herbal infusion using the guidance for that blend and give the botanicals time to develop their aroma, colour and flavour.

Step 02

Play

Bring atmosphere into the ritual. Use the official RastaRoots playlist or choose music that fits the moment you want to create.

Step 03

Pause

Let the cup interrupt the rush. Put unnecessary distractions aside and notice the temperature, aroma and flavour rather than drinking automatically.

Step 04

Root

The end point is connection — to yourself, another person, family, culture, nature or simply the present moment.

Tea Ritual Explained

Herbal tea, botanicals and ritual — clearly explained.

Quick answers about the RastaRoots approach to herbal tea rituals, the five blends and the plants behind them.

What is a tea ritual?

A tea ritual is a deliberate way of preparing and drinking tea that gives attention to the process as well as the drink.

What is the RastaRoots Tea Ritual?

It is a modern RastaRoots framework combining herbal tea, preparation, music and intentional time through Rise, Root, Bloom, Endure and Unwind.

How do I create a herbal tea ritual?

Choose the moment, select a suitable herbal infusion, prepare it according to the blend guidance, add music if you want it, pause while the herbs infuse and drink with attention.

Is this a traditional tea ceremony?

No. Tea Ritual is a modern RastaRoots ritual framework and is not presented as a traditional Japanese, Chinese or other formal tea ceremony.

What herbal teas are in RastaRoots?

Roots Fire, Bush Doctor, Empress Bloom, Iron Root and Cool Mind combine botanicals including ginger, hibiscus, mint, moringa, lemongrass, cerasee, bitter melon and sarsaparilla.

Are herbal teas caffeine-free?

Many pure herbal infusions are naturally caffeine-free because they do not contain traditional tea leaves. Check the ingredients of each individual blend.

Which blend contains ginger?

Roots Fire combines ginger, lemongrass and mint. Iron Root also contains ginger alongside sarsaparilla and moringa.

Which blend contains hibiscus?

Empress Bloom contains hibiscus or sorrel with mint and moringa. Cool Mind also contains hibiscus alongside mint and lemongrass.
