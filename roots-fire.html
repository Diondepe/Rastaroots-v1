Why Roots Fire Exists

Some mornings need more than another rushed drink.

Roots Fire was created as a deliberate morning ritual: warming, aromatic and full of character. It brings together three familiar herbs with contrasting flavour profiles—spice, citrus and cooling freshness—to create a cup that feels bold without relying on artificial flavouring or excessive sweetness.

What’s Inside

Three ingredients. One rooted blend.

Each ingredient contributes a distinct flavour, aroma and cultural connection. The information below describes flavour and traditional context rather than promising medical outcomes.

The Roots Fire Ritual

Brew. Breathe. Begin.

Roots Fire is more than the contents of the sachet. The complete ritual combines preparation, music and a short moment of intention before the demands of the day begin.

STEP 01

Prepare

Place one Roots Fire sachet into your cup and prepare it according to the brewing instructions supplied with the collection.

STEP 02

Pause

Allow the infusion to develop. Put the phone down and give the ritual your attention rather than treating it as another task.

STEP 03

Listen

Open the RastaRoots Tea Ritual playlist and pair the cup with rooted rhythms selected for reflection, movement and connection.

STEP 04

Begin

Choose one intention for the day. Drink slowly, move deliberately and carry that intention into what comes next.
