Meet The Guardian

Every beginning needs intention.

The Roots Fire Guardian represents movement, discipline and the decision to begin. He does not wait for perfect conditions. He understands that direction comes from knowing what you are moving towards — and remembering what you are carrying with you.

The Story Behind The Blend

Begin deliberately.

Roots Fire was created as the opening ritual of the RastaRoots Discovery Collection. Ginger provides the warmth and spice, lemongrass brings brightness, and mint gives the infusion its clean, cooling finish.

The blend represents the moment before movement — when you decide how you want to enter the day rather than simply allowing the day to decide for you.

What’s Inside

Three botanicals. One warming ritual.

Roots Fire combines warming spice, citrus aroma and cooling freshness. The information below describes flavour and cultural context rather than promising medical outcomes.

The Roots Fire Ritual

Brew. Breathe. Begin.

Roots Fire is designed as a morning ritual: a simple opportunity to stop before the noise begins and decide how you want to enter the day.

STEP 01

Prepare

Place one Roots Fire sachet into your cup and prepare it according to the brewing guidance supplied with the collection.

STEP 02

Notice

Let the infusion develop. Notice the ginger warmth, citrus aroma and cooling mint rather than treating preparation as another task to rush through.

STEP 03

Listen

Play the RastaRoots Tea Ritual playlist and create a short space for movement, reflection or preparation for what comes next.

STEP 04

Begin

Choose one intention for the day. Finish the cup, move deliberately and carry that intention into your next action.
