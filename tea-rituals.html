Your guide

What to know before you choose

1. Rise — a morning beginning

Choose Roots Fire if you enjoy ginger warmth, citrus-like lemongrass and refreshing mint. Prepare your cup before the day accelerates. Notice the aroma and set one intention for the morning.

2. Root — reconnect with heritage

Bush Doctor brings cerasee, bitter melon and moringa into a distinctive bitter profile. Explore the stories behind these plants through the Herbal Library and the Caribbean Bush Tea guide; traditions differ across families and islands.

3. Bloom — make room for creativity

Empress Bloom combines hibiscus, mint and moringa. Its floral and tart character can accompany journaling, reading or a quiet creative break. There is no prescribed way to enjoy the moment.

4. Endure — reset between tasks

Iron Root is led by sarsaparilla, ginger and moringa. Make a cup between activities, not as a performance or endurance aid, but as a clear transition from one part of the day to another.

5. Unwind — close the day deliberately

Cool Mind brings together mint, lemongrass and hibiscus. Put your phone aside, choose some music and prepare the infusion according to the label. This is a relaxation ritual, not a treatment for anxiety or insomnia.

Frequently asked questions

What is a tea ritual?

A repeatable, intentional moment built around preparing and enjoying tea. It can include music, reflection or conversation.

Do I need special equipment?

No. A suitable cup, hot water and the preparation instructions for your tea are enough.

Are tea rituals medical treatments?

No. They are lifestyle practices and should not replace professional care.

Can I explore all five rituals?

Yes. The Founder Discovery Collection contains five sachets of each of the five blends.
