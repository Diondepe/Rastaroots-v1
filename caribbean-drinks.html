Quick answer

What are the classic Caribbean and African drinks?

The best-known are sorrel (spiced hibiscus), ginger beer, Guinness punch and peanut punch from the Caribbean, and zobo, bissap and sobolo from West Africa, which are made from the same hibiscus flower as sorrel. Many start with a plant you can read about in our Herbal Library.

08 • Questions People Ask

Caribbean and African drinks, answered.

What are traditional Caribbean drinks?
Classic Caribbean drinks include sorrel (spiced hibiscus), ginger beer, mauby, Guinness punch, peanut punch, Irish moss, carrot juice and cocoa tea, plus malt drinks and fruit punches.
What is the difference between sorrel, zobo and bissap?
They are all made from the same flower, Hibiscus sabdariffa. Sorrel is the Caribbean Christmas version with pimento and ginger, zobo is Nigerian with pineapple and ginger, and bissap is Senegalese, often with mint and vanilla.
What are popular Jamaican drinks?
Popular Jamaican drinks include sorrel, ginger beer, rum punch, Guinness punch, peanut punch, Irish moss, carrot juice, soursop juice and malt drinks.
What do Jamaicans drink at Christmas?
Sorrel and homemade ginger beer are the classic Jamaican Christmas drinks, served with black cake. Many families add a little rum for adults.
