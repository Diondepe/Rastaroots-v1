Meet The Guardian

Knowledge comes before the claim.

The Bush Doctor Guardian represents plant knowledge, patience and respect for tradition. He understands that knowing a herb means more than repeating what someone says it can do. It means knowing the plant, its history, its preparation, its flavour and the context in which that knowledge was carried.

“Know the plant before you ask what the plant can do.”

The Story Behind The Blend

Not everything valuable was made to taste sweet.

Bush Doctor is the most uncompromising blend in the Discovery Collection. Cerasee and bitter melon create the distinctive bitter character, while moringa provides a softer leafy foundation beneath them.

The blend is inspired by the Caribbean tradition of bush tea and the generations who learned plants through observation, family knowledge and lived experience — long before herbal wellness became a modern industry.

What’s Inside

Three botanicals. One uncompromising blend.

Bush Doctor is intentionally bitter. The information below describes flavour and traditional context rather than promising medical outcomes.

Ingredient 01

Cerasee

Cerasee is strongly associated with Caribbean bush-tea traditions and is recognised for its unmistakably bitter character. It gives Bush Doctor much of its distinctive identity.

Taste

Bitter, green and earthy.

Flavour Profile

Bitter by design.

Bush Doctor begins with the unmistakable bitterness of cerasee and bitter melon before developing into a greener, earthier moringa finish. It is intentionally challenging and deliberately different from sweeter everyday infusions.

The Bush Doctor Ritual

Brew. Taste. Remember.

Bush Doctor is designed as a slower ritual — a reminder that not every meaningful experience needs to be softened, sweetened or made instantly comfortable.

STEP 01

Prepare

Place one Bush Doctor sachet into your cup and prepare it according to the brewing guidance supplied with the collection.

STEP 02

Notice

Allow the bitterness to develop fully. Notice how the green, earthy and bitter notes change as the infusion rests.

STEP 03

Listen

Play the RastaRoots Tea Ritual playlist and use the moment for quiet reflection, reading, conversation or simply being still.

STEP 04

Remember

Think about one piece of family, cultural or practical knowledge worth carrying forward rather than allowing it to disappear.
