Meet The Guardian

The deepest knowledge is rarely the loudest.

The Guardian represents the responsibility to listen before speaking, learn before claiming and protect the knowledge that has travelled through families and communities. Bush Doctor honours the elders who understood plants through experience, observation and traditions passed forward over time.

“Bitterness teaches patience. Wisdom is not always wrapped in sweetness.”

The Story Behind The Name

The Bush Doctor understood what grew around them.

Across Caribbean communities, plant knowledge was often carried by elders, growers and family members who understood the leaves, roots and preparations used within everyday life.

Bush Doctor is not presented as a medical title or a promise of treatment. It is a cultural tribute to the people who preserved practical plant knowledge and shared it with their communities.

What’s Inside

Three bold herbs. One uncompromising ritual.

The Bush Doctor Ritual

Brew. Listen. Understand.

Bush Doctor is a ritual for slowing down and paying attention. Its naturally bitter flavour encourages you to sip deliberately rather than rushing through the cup.

STEP 01

Prepare

Place one Bush Doctor sachet into your cup and prepare it according to the brewing instructions supplied with the collection.

STEP 02

Observe

Notice the deep green colour and herbal aroma as the ingredients infuse. Allow the cup time to develop its full character.

STEP 03

Listen

Open the RastaRoots Tea Ritual playlist and create a quiet space where you can experience the blend without distraction.

STEP 04

Reflect

Consider one lesson or piece of knowledge you want to preserve, practise or pass forward to someone else.
