Caribbean Peanut Punch

Creamy • Nutty • Nutmeg-Spiced

Thick, nutty and naturally filling: peanut punch is sold at roadside stalls from Kingston to Port of Spain. Here is the easy home version.

Get The Recipe ↓ More Drinks

Quick answer

How do you make peanut punch?

Blend 4 tbsp smooth peanut butter (or 100 g roasted unsalted peanuts) with 500 ml cold milk, 4 to 6 tbsp condensed milk, 1 tsp vanilla and a grating of nutmeg until smooth and frothy. Serve over ice. It contains peanuts, so keep it well away from anyone with a nut allergy.

The recipe in five steps

  1. Add peanut butter or peanuts to a blender.
  2. Pour in cold milk and condensed milk.
  3. Add vanilla, nutmeg and cinnamon.
  4. Blend for 1 minute until smooth.
  5. Serve over ice.

01 • Ingredients

What you need.

01 • Peanuts

4 tbsp smooth peanut butter

Or 100 g roasted unsalted peanuts, blended with a splash of milk first.

02 • Milk

500 ml cold milk

Whole milk is creamiest. Oat or soya milk make it dairy-free.

03 • Sweet

4–6 tbsp condensed milk

Or honey or sugar to taste.

04 • Spice

Vanilla, nutmeg, cinnamon

1 tsp vanilla, ¼ tsp grated nutmeg and a pinch of cinnamon.

02 • Method

Step by step.

Classic peanut punch

If you are using whole peanuts, blend them with a splash of the milk into a smooth paste first.

Add the peanut butter or paste, cold milk and condensed milk to the blender.

Add the vanilla, nutmeg and cinnamon.

Blend for about a minute until completely smooth and frothy. Taste and adjust the sweetness.

Pour over ice and grate a little more nutmeg on top. Drink the same day.

03 • Variations

Make it your own.

Dairy-Free

Plant peanut punch

Use oat or soya milk and sweeten with maple syrup or a condensed oat milk.

Thicker

Oats and peanut

Blend in 2 tbsp porridge oats for a breakfast-style punch.

Trinidad

Bottled-style punch

Add a little extra condensed milk and a pinch of salt for the sweet, salty roadside style.

Frozen

Peanut punch lollies

Freeze in lolly moulds for a cold treat.

Stout

Peanut and stout

Blend with a splash of stout for an adults-only Sunday punch.

06 • Make It Safely

Safety, allergens and storage.

Good to know before you make it.

Peanut allergy: Peanut allergy can be severe. Never serve this to anyone with a peanut or nut allergy, and wash the blender well afterwards.

Allergens: Contains peanuts and milk, and gluten if you add oats. Check labels on peanut butter and condensed milk.

Sugar: Condensed milk is very sweet. Start with less and add to taste.

Storage: Drink on the day it is made and keep it in the fridge.

07 • Questions People Ask

Your questions, answered.

What is peanut punch?
A creamy Caribbean drink made by blending peanuts or peanut butter with milk, a sweetener like condensed milk, and spices such as nutmeg and vanilla.
Is peanut punch healthy?
It is a filling treat rather than a health drink. Peanuts add protein, but condensed milk adds a lot of sugar, so make it less sweet at home.
Can I make peanut punch without condensed milk?
Yes. Sweeten with honey, maple syrup or sugar, or use a sweetened plant milk.
Is peanut punch Jamaican or Trinidadian?
Both. It is popular across the Caribbean, from Jamaican street vendors to Trinidadian bottled punch.
How long does homemade peanut punch last?
It is best fresh. Keep it in the fridge and drink it the same day.
