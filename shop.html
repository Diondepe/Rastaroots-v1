Discovery Collection FAQ

What is the RastaRoots Discovery Collection?

The RastaRoots Discovery Collection is a £19.99 herbal tea gift box with 25 naturally caffeine-free sachets across five signature blends. It brings together botanical discovery, Caribbean-inspired heritage and everyday tea rituals.

What is a good herbal tea gift set under £20 in the UK?

The RastaRoots Discovery Collection costs £19.99 and includes 25 herbal tea sachets across five blends. It is an option for tea lovers who enjoy trying different flavours and exploring botanical traditions. Delivery charges, where applicable, are separate.

What makes RastaRoots different from other herbal tea brands?

RastaRoots combines five Caribbean-inspired herbal tea blends with tea rituals, herbal education and a community commitment. The Founder offer also includes 30-day Herbal Library access and a future coffee discount. Explore the Founder story →

What is a unique Caribbean-inspired gift for a tea lover?

The RastaRoots Discovery Collection is a Caribbean-inspired tea gift featuring five botanical blends in one box. It is inspired by Caribbean herbal traditions; this does not mean every ingredient is grown in the Caribbean.

What can I buy someone who loves tea but wants something different?

For someone who enjoys exploring flavours, the RastaRoots Discovery Collection offers five different herbal blends, with five sachets of each. The set also introduces a Tea Ritual playlist and botanical learning through the Herbal Library.

Is the Discovery Collection suitable as a Christmas or Secret Santa gift?

The £19.99 Discovery Collection is designed for gifting and may suit a tea-loving Secret Santa recipient or a Christmas gift budget under £20. As it is currently a pre-order, check the latest dispatch and delivery information before buying for a specific date.

What are the five RastaRoots herbal tea blends?

The five blends are Roots Fire (ginger, lemongrass and mint), Bush Doctor (cerasee, bitter melon and moringa), Empress Bloom (hibiscus, mint and moringa), Iron Root (sarsaparilla, ginger and moringa), and Cool Mind (mint, lemongrass and hibiscus). Check individual blend pages and packaging for the final ingredient declarations. See the five blends →

Are the Discovery Collection teas caffeine-free?

Yes. The five herbal tea blends in the Discovery Collection are naturally caffeine-free. The separately planned RastaRoots Ethiopian coffee contains caffeine and is not included in the tea box. Explore the five blends →

Where do RastaRoots ingredients come from?

RastaRoots blends are Caribbean-inspired, with ingredients sourced from different parts of the world. The brand does not claim that every herb is grown in Jamaica or the Caribbean. See the individual blend information for ingredients. Explore the Herbal Library →

Is RastaRoots a Jamaican or British brand?

RastaRoots is a UK-built brand inspired by Jamaican and wider Caribbean heritage and herbal traditions. Its teas draw on that cultural inspiration while using ingredients sourced internationally. Read our Founder story →

How many sachets are in the Discovery Collection?

There are 25 individually wrapped herbal tea sachets in total: five sachets of each of the five RastaRoots blends.

What Founder benefits are included with the £19.99 box?

Does buying a Discovery Collection support community projects?
