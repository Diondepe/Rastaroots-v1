The Discovery Collection contains 25 herbal tea sachets across five signature RastaRoots rituals, with five sachets per blend, plus access to the RastaRoots Tea Ritual playlist.

What are the five RastaRoots herbal tea blends?

The five blends are Roots Fire, Bush Doctor, Empress Bloom, Iron Root and Cool Mind. Each blend has its own ingredients, flavour profile and ritual.

Are the teas caffeine free?

Yes. The RastaRoots Discovery Collection is naturally caffeine free.

How much is the Discovery Collection?

The Founder Discovery Collection is currently available for £19.99.

What Founder extras do I receive?

Founder customers receive 30 days of Herbal Library access and 15% off RastaRoots Coffee alongside the Discovery Collection.

The Herbal Library is RastaRoots' educational space for exploring the plants, ingredients, traditions and stories behind the rituals. Discovery Collection customers receive 30 days of access as part of the Founder offer.

Does buying a Discovery Collection support community projects?

Yes. £1 from every Discovery Collection sold is committed to the RastaRoots Build Our Communities Back fund for grassroots community projects.

Where do I complete payment?

Checkout is completed securely through the official RastaRoots Shopify store.
