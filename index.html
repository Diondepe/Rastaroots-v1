RastaRoots Explained

Herbal tea, bush tea, botanical knowledge and coffee.

Clear answers about the drinks, plants and rituals behind RastaRoots.

What is RastaRoots?

RastaRoots is a British-Caribbean lifestyle brand built around herbal tea, botanical knowledge, Tea Ritual, coffee, culture and community.

What is herbal tea?

Herbal tea, also called a herbal infusion or tisane, is made by steeping botanical ingredients such as roots, leaves, flowers, fruit or seeds in water instead of traditional tea leaves.

Does herbal tea have caffeine?

Many pure herbal infusions are naturally caffeine-free because they contain no traditional tea leaves. Always check the ingredient list for the individual blend.

What is Caribbean bush tea?

Bush tea is a broad Caribbean term for traditional herbal infusions made from locally known leaves, roots and other plants. Recipes and knowledge vary between islands, communities and families.

What is a RastaRoots Tea Ritual?

A RastaRoots Tea Ritual combines herbal tea preparation with intentional time and music. The five ritual themes are Rise, Root, Bloom, Endure and Unwind.

How do you make herbal tea?

Herbal tea is generally made by infusing appropriate botanical ingredients in hot water for a suitable amount of time. Preparation varies by plant, so RastaRoots links individual preparation guidance to the Herbal Library rather than treating every herb the same.

What is in the Discovery Collection?

The Founder Discovery Collection contains 25 herbal tea sachets across five blends: Roots Fire, Bush Doctor, Empress Bloom, Iron Root and Cool Mind.

What is the Herbal Library?

It is a growing educational botanical archive covering identity, flavour, preparation, heritage, traditional knowledge, research and safety. Eight guides are live and the Library is planned to grow to 53 plants.

Is RastaRoots launching Ethiopian coffee?

Yes. Coffee is the next product chapter, beginning with a 100% Ethiopian Arabica freeze-dried instant coffee concept focused on origin, flavour, convenience and ritual.
