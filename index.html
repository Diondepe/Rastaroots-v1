What is Caribbean bush tea?

Across the Caribbean, “bush tea” is a broad term for herbal infusions made using familiar leaves, roots and other plants. Recipes, preparation methods and meanings differ across islands and families. Explore how cerasee, ginger, sarsaparilla and sorrel feature in different traditions.

Frequently asked questions about Caribbean herbal tea

What is the difference between herbal tea and regular tea?

Regular tea comes from the tea plant, Camellia sinensis. Herbal teas, also called herbal infusions or tisanes, use other plants such as hibiscus flowers, mint leaves or ginger root. Most herbal infusions made only from these botanicals are naturally caffeine-free.

Is Jamaican bush tea one specific recipe?

No. “Bush tea” describes a range of plant-based infusions rather than one fixed recipe. The plants used and the knowledge passed down can differ by household, island and community. Cerasee is one plant associated with Jamaican bush-tea traditions.

What is sorrel tea in the Caribbean?

Caribbean sorrel is commonly made from the calyces of Hibiscus sabdariffa, often prepared as a richly coloured drink. Recipes vary and may include spices. Explore the hibiscus and sorrel guide for plant identity and preparation traditions.

Are cerasee and bitter melon the same plant?

The names can refer to Momordica charantia, but different traditions may use different plant parts and preparations. The fruit is often called bitter melon, while Caribbean cerasee bush tea is commonly associated with the vine or leaves. Read our cerasee guide and bitter melon guide.

Which RastaRoots herbal tea should I try first?

For a warming, spicy cup explore Roots Fire; for a bitter botanical tradition try Bush Doctor; for a floral infusion choose Empress Bloom; for an earthy root-led flavour explore Iron Root; and for a mint-forward cup try Cool Mind. The five tea rituals help you compare ingredients and flavour.

Can herbal tea replace medical treatment?

No. Herbal tea is a beverage, not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment. Herbs can interact with medicines and may be unsuitable during pregnancy or for some health conditions. Consult an appropriate healthcare professional if you are unsure.

What is RastaRoots?

RastaRoots is a British-Caribbean lifestyle brand built around herbal tea, botanical knowledge, Tea Ritual, culture and community.

What is herbal tea?

Herbal tea is an infusion made from botanical ingredients such as roots, leaves, flowers, fruit or seeds rather than traditional tea leaves.

What is Caribbean bush tea?

Bush tea is a broad Caribbean term for traditional herbal infusions made from locally known plants. Practices vary between islands, communities and families.
