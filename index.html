RastaRoots Explained

Herbal tea, botanicals, ritual and roots.

Clear answers about the plants, products and ideas behind RastaRoots.

What is RastaRoots?

RastaRoots is a British-Caribbean lifestyle brand built around herbal tea, botanical knowledge, Tea Ritual, culture and community.

What is herbal tea?

Herbal tea is an infusion made from botanical ingredients such as roots, leaves, flowers, fruit or seeds rather than traditional tea leaves.

What is Caribbean bush tea?

Bush tea is a broad Caribbean term for traditional herbal infusions made from locally known plants. Practices vary between islands, communities and families.

Which eight herbs can I explore?

Ginger, lemongrass, mint, hibiscus or sorrel, moringa, cerasee, bitter melon and sarsaparilla form the founding eight guides.

What is a Tea Ritual?

A RastaRoots Tea Ritual combines herbal tea preparation with intentional time and music.

What is the Herbal Library?

It is a growing botanical archive covering identity, flavour, preparation, heritage, traditional knowledge, research and safety.
