What Is A Tea Ritual?

Tea already asks for time. Make the time mean something.

A RastaRoots Tea Ritual is our modern framework for combining herbal tea preparation, music and intentional time.

It is not presented as a traditional Japanese, Chinese or other formal tea ceremony. It is a RastaRoots approach to making an everyday cup more deliberate.

RastaRoots Explained

Herbal tea, coffee, botanical knowledge and ritual.

Clear answers about what RastaRoots is building and how the pieces connect.

What is RastaRoots?

RastaRoots is a British-Caribbean lifestyle brand built around herbal tea, coffee, botanical knowledge, ritual, culture and community.

What is herbal tea?

Herbal tea, often called a herbal infusion, is a drink made by steeping botanical ingredients such as roots, leaves, flowers, fruit or seeds in water rather than using traditional tea leaves.

What is a RastaRoots Tea Ritual?

A RastaRoots Tea Ritual combines herbal tea preparation with intentional time and music. The five signature rituals are Rise, Root, Bloom, Endure and Unwind.

What is in the Discovery Collection?

The Founder Discovery Collection contains 25 sachets across five herbal blends: Roots Fire, Bush Doctor, Empress Bloom, Iron Root and Cool Mind.

What is the Herbal Library?

It is a growing educational archive exploring botanical identity, flavour, preparation, heritage, traditional knowledge, research and safety. Eight founding guides are live and the collection is planned to grow to 53 plants.

Is RastaRoots launching coffee?

Yes. Coffee is the next product chapter, beginning with a 100% Ethiopian Arabica freeze-dried instant coffee concept focused on origin, flavour, preparation and ritual.
