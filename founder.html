Why I Started

I wanted to build something with meaning.

RastaRoots started from a simple frustration: too much of modern wellness feels disconnected from culture, history and the people who carried knowledge long before it became a trend.

I wanted to create something different — a brand where herbal ingredients, music, family, heritage, nature and daily ritual could exist together.

Tea became the first product because a cup gives us something rare: a reason to stop for a few minutes.

FROM ROOTZ TO REALITY

Not just a brand story. A room full of people.

At our Manchester RastaRoots tasting event, I stood in front of the community to explain why this brand matters to me. People came together to discover the teas, explore the blends and have real conversations about culture, herbal traditions and what we want to build next.

Seeing people take the time to listen, taste and ask questions reminded me that RastaRoots must always be about more than what we sell. The community is part of the journey, not an afterthought.

The Bigger Idea

The product may be tea. The mission is reconnection.

RastaRoots is not being built around the idea that one herbal drink can solve your life. It is being built around something simpler: knowing what you consume, creating better rituals and remaining connected to the culture, people and values that shaped you.

That is why the brand now reaches beyond the cup into herbal education, music, storytelling, nature, community and identity.

Built in Manchester and inspired by Caribbean heritage, RastaRoots draws on traditions of sharing plant knowledge through families and communities. Our botanical ingredients are sourced from different parts of the world; Caribbean-inspired describes the culture and creative direction, not a claim that every ingredient is grown in Jamaica.

The Philosophy

Don’t Lose Your Rootz.

Roots are not about living in the past. They are about understanding what holds you steady while you move forward.

Your family. Your culture. Your values. Your stories. Your traditions. The places that shaped you. The knowledge passed down before you were here.

Progress matters. But progress without memory can leave people with more choices and less connection.

“Know where you come from. Decide where you are going. Don’t lose yourself between the two.”

Why RastaRoots Exists

Three things I believe are worth protecting.

01 • Knowledge

Know what is in your cup.

Herbal ingredients deserve context. Their flavour, origins, traditional use and modern evidence should be understood rather than reduced to exaggerated claims on social media.

02 • Ritual

Give ordinary moments meaning.

A kettle, a cup and a few minutes can become an intentional break from constant noise. Tea Ritual was created around that idea.

03 • Roots

Carry culture forward.

Heritage should not be treated as decoration. It should remain connected to people, stories, music, food, plants, values and community.

Tea Ritual

Brew. Sip. Root. Repeat.

Tea Ritual is where the philosophy becomes something practical. Choose the blend that fits the moment, prepare it deliberately, put on the RastaRoots playlist and give your attention somewhere worth keeping.

It does not need to be complicated. The point is not perfection. The point is presence.

Founder Principles

The standards I want the brand to keep as it grows.

Growth should make RastaRoots more capable — not less rooted.

01

Culture before trend.

We should know why something belongs in the RastaRoots world before using it because it is fashionable.

02

Evidence before hype.

Traditional knowledge deserves respect, but health claims should not be exaggerated simply because they sell.

03

Quality before scale.

Growth means very little if the customer experience, ingredients or brand standards become weaker.

04

Purpose before noise.

Not every piece of content, product or opportunity needs to be chased. The brand should remain clear about what it stands for.

FOUNDER QUESTIONS

The story, in your words.

Who founded RastaRoots?

RastaRoots Life was founded by Dion Depeiaza. The brand is being built in Manchester around herbal tea, Caribbean-inspired culture, education, everyday ritual and community.

Where is RastaRoots based?

RastaRoots is a Manchester-built British brand inspired by Caribbean heritage. Its botanical ingredients are sourced internationally; Caribbean-inspired does not mean all ingredients are grown in Jamaica.

Why was RastaRoots created?

RastaRoots was created to reconnect herbal products with the traditions, stories and people behind them. Its philosophy, “Don’t Lose Your Rootz”, encourages people to carry their heritage and values forward.

What does Don’t Lose Your Rootz mean?

Don’t Lose Your Rootz is the RastaRoots philosophy of remembering the people, culture, knowledge and values that shaped you while continuing to grow.

What happened at the RastaRoots tea-tasting event?

The Manchester tea-tasting session brought people together to try RastaRoots teas, hear the founder explain the brand and share feedback. It is part of building the brand through real conversations.

What does the RastaRoots Discovery Collection include?

The £19.99 Founder Discovery Collection contains 25 naturally caffeine-free herbal tea sachets across five blends: Roots Fire, Bush Doctor, Empress Bloom, Iron Root and Cool Mind.

How does RastaRoots support community projects?

RastaRoots commits £1 from every Discovery Collection sold to its Build Our Communities Back fund, intended to support grassroots community initiatives. Read the community page for the evolving mission.

Where can I learn about the herbs used in RastaRoots teas?

The RastaRoots Herbal Library introduces botanical ingredients, their cultural and traditional context, preparation and modern evidence. It is educational and does not replace medical advice.
