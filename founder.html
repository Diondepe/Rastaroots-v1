My Story

I wanted to build something with meaning.

RastaRoots Life started from a simple idea: wellness should feel personal, cultural and rooted. Not cold. Not empty. Not just another product on a shelf.

I wanted to create a brand that connects natural herbal tea with daily routine, music, family, history, reflection and community.

For me, this is about remembering where we come from while building something powerful for where we are going.

Why I Started RastaRoots

Because people need rituals that reconnect them.

Life moves fast. Work, pressure, phones, stress and noise can pull people away from themselves. RastaRoots is a reminder to slow down, brew something natural, listen to something soulful and come back to your centre.

The tea is the product. But the ritual is the real experience.

The Vision

A lifestyle brand rooted in wellness, culture and community.

RastaRoots Life is being built for people who care about what they consume, how they live and what they stand for.

The long-term goal is to build a premium herbal tea lifestyle brand that can sit proudly beside major wellness brands while staying rooted in Caribbean culture, family values and community purpose.

Tea should not just be consumed. It should create a moment.
