Meet The Guardian

Growth begins beneath the surface.

The Guardian represents the knowledge that strength and softness can exist together. Empress Bloom celebrates the process of becoming: developing quietly, recognising your own value and allowing your character to emerge without needing permission.

“Do not rush the bloom. Strong roots are doing their work beneath the surface.”

The Story Behind The Blend

Vibrance is not the same as noise.

Empress Bloom was created as a colourful, refreshing expression of the RastaRoots collection. Hibiscus provides the bold colour and tart floral character, while mint introduces freshness and moringa adds a smoother herbal foundation.

The name represents presence, self-respect and the confidence to grow into your own identity without becoming disconnected from your roots.

What’s Inside

Three botanicals. One vibrant ritual.

Empress Bloom combines floral tartness, cooling freshness and mild leafy depth. The information below describes flavour and cultural context rather than promising medical outcomes.

✦
Ingredient 01

Hibiscus / Sorrel

Hibiscus gives Empress Bloom its vivid colour and distinctive tart flavour. Sorrel drinks have an important place within Caribbean food traditions, particularly around celebrations and family gatherings.

Taste

Tart, floral and fruit-forward.

Flavour Profile

Tart florals. Cooling freshness.

Empress Bloom opens with the distinctive tartness of hibiscus, develops into cooling mint and finishes with the mild leafy character of moringa. It is colourful, aromatic and refreshing without becoming excessively sweet.

The Empress Bloom Ritual

Brew. Pause. Bloom.

Empress Bloom is designed for a refreshing pause: a moment to step back from noise, reconnect with yourself and recognise the progress that may not yet be visible to everyone else.

STEP 01

Prepare

Place one Empress Bloom sachet into your cup and prepare it according to the brewing instructions supplied with the collection.

STEP 02

Notice

Watch the colour develop and take in the floral aroma. Give the blend time to reveal its full character.

STEP 03

Listen

Play the RastaRoots Tea Ritual playlist and create a short space for calm, creativity and intentional reflection.

STEP 04

Bloom

Recognise one part of yourself or your journey that deserves more patience, attention or appreciation.
