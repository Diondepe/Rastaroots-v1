The Rootz Library Promise

Knowledge without the hype.

The Rootz Library exists to explore plants with respect for their origins, cultural traditions and modern evidence.

Traditional knowledge matters. Scientific evidence matters. They are not always the same thing, and the Library will always be clear about the difference.

“Before you ask what a plant can do for you, learn where it came from.”
