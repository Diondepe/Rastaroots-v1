West Indian Bay Leaf

Pimenta racemosa

Spicy • Clove-like • Warm

Bwa denn in Dominica, the bay rum tree across the islands. The clove-scented Caribbean leaf behind bay rum and bay oil, how it is used as bush tea, and why it is not the same as kitchen bay.

Quick answer

What is West Indian bay leaf?

West Indian bay leaf comes from the bay rum tree (Pimenta racemosa), a cousin of allspice native to the Caribbean. Its leaves smell strongly of clove and are used in cooking and bush tea, and distilled into bay oil and bay rum, a famous cologne. It is a different plant from the bay laurel sold in UK supermarkets. There is little research on bay leaf tea.

Side effects & who should take care

  • Use only a leaf or two in tea or cooking.
  • Bay oil and bay rum are toxic: never drink them.
  • Not enough is known about medicinal use in pregnancy.
  • Remove whole leaves before eating; they can be a choking hazard.

West Indian Bay Leaf At A Glance

What is West Indian bay?

West Indian bay is an evergreen tree in the myrtle family, related to allspice (pimento). Its glossy leaves are full of aromatic oil.

Botanical Name Pimenta racemosa
Family Myrtaceae (myrtle family)
Part Used Leaves
Character Spicy, clove-like
Native Range The Caribbean and northern South America

West Indian bay, Dominica and the evidence

West Indian bay grows naturally throughout the Caribbean.

Dominica is famous for its bay oil, distilled from the leaves by rural farmers.

Bay leaf tea is a Caribbean bush remedy, with little scientific research.

01 • Know The Leaf

Not your supermarket bay.

West Indian bay is a tree with thick, glossy, oval leaves and small white flowers.

Its leaf oil is rich in eugenol, the compound that gives cloves their smell.

Kitchen bay leaves in the UK come from bay laurel (Laurus nobilis), a different plant with a cooler, eucalyptus-like scent.

It is a close cousin of Jamaica’s pimento, which gives allspice.

The scent of the islands in a single leaf.

02 • Bay Leaf Uses

What is West Indian bay leaf used for?

West Indian bay is used in cooking, perfume and bush medicine.

The honest picture: a beloved Caribbean leaf, little research.

In Dominica and elsewhere, bay leaf tea is used to soothe stomach upset, ease aches and help sleep. Lab and animal studies suggest the leaf oil has pain-relieving and antimicrobial effects, but there is little human research on bay leaf tea. The oil itself is toxic to swallow.

04 • Caribbean Heritage

Dominica’s scented tree.

West Indian bay grows naturally across the Caribbean and northern South America.

Bay rum, first made by distilling the leaves with rum, probably began in the Virgin Islands and became a popular cologne and hair tonic.

In Dominica, where the tree is called bwa denn, rural farmers still distil bay oil, one of the island’s best-known exports.

10 • How To Make Bay Leaf Tea

How to make West Indian bay leaf tea.

One leaf is plenty. It is strong.

West Indian bay leaf tea

Rinse 1 to 2 fresh or dried West Indian bay leaves and tear them slightly.

Pour over 250 ml of just-boiled water.

Cover and steep for 5 minutes, then remove the leaves.

Add honey or a slice of ginger. Never add bay oil.

Step 02 • Pour 250 ml just-boiled water.
Step 03 • Steep 5 minutes, covered.

11 • West Indian Bay Leaf Side Effects & Safety

A leaf is fine. The oil is not.

Oil, pregnancy and choking.

A leaf or two in tea or cooking is generally safe for most adults.

Bay oil and bay rum: the concentrated oil is toxic, and bay rum is not for drinking. Keep them away from children.

Pregnancy: there is not enough information on medicinal use, so stick to food amounts.

Whole leaves: remove them before eating, as they are tough and can be a choking hazard.

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication, preparing for surgery or managing a medical condition, speak to a pharmacist before using bay leaf as a remedy, and get urgent help if anyone swallows bay oil.

12 • Questions People Ask About West Indian Bay Leaf

West Indian bay leaf questions, answered clearly.

Straightforward answers to the questions people search for most.

What is West Indian bay leaf?
The leaf of the bay rum tree, Pimenta racemosa, native to the Caribbean.
Is West Indian bay the same as bay laurel?
No. Bay laurel (Laurus nobilis) is a different plant with a different scent.
How do you make bay leaf tea?
Steep 1 to 2 leaves in 250 ml of just-boiled water for 5 minutes, then remove them.
What is bay rum?
A cologne and hair tonic made by distilling bay leaves with rum. Do not drink it.
Is bay oil safe to drink?
No. Bay oil is toxic if swallowed.
What is bay leaf tea used for in the Caribbean?
Traditionally for stomach upset, aches and sleep. There is little research.
Is it related to allspice?
Yes. It is a close cousin of pimento, the source of allspice.
Is bay leaf tea safe in pregnancy?
Food amounts are fine. Avoid medicinal amounts.
Where can I buy West Indian bay leaves in the UK?
In some Caribbean grocers, often sold dried.
Does bay leaf tea contain caffeine?
No. It is caffeine-free.
